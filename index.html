DISCORD COLLECTIBLE CARD GAME

Collect. Discover. Build your collection.

Soulwon is a Discord-based collectible card game where players collect cards, discover different rarities, and build their own personal collection.

ABOUT SOULWON

A collection built for Discord.

Soulwon turns collecting into a Discord experience. Players can obtain cards, manage their collection, trade and interact with other players, and chase rarer cards and unique prints.

COLLECTION SYSTEM

More than just cards.

01

Rarities

Discover cards across Soulwon's rarity system, from Wolf through Genesis.

02

Prints

Cards receive individual print numbers, giving collectors another way to chase and compare their cards.

Build your collection, organize your cards, and show off what you've collected.
