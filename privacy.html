Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Soulwon may collect, use, and retain information when you use the Soulwon Discord bot, website, and related game services.

Soulwon is a Discord-based collectible card game. Information associated with your Discord account may be used to provide and maintain your game progress and the features of the service.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Soulwon may process information associated with your use of the service, including:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord server or guild ID
  • Your Soulwon player profile and game progress
  • Cards and card collections
  • Card print numbers
  • In-game currencies and balances
  • Cooldowns and other game-state information
  • Battle and gameplay statistics
  • Organization or group information associated with your Soulwon account
  • Other information required to provide specific Soulwon features

Soulwon generally uses Discord identifiers to associate game data with the appropriate player and to provide the service through Discord.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Soulwon's game features
  • Maintain player accounts and game progress
  • Store collections, cards, prints, currencies, and other game data
  • Enable interactions between players
  • Operate battles, events, organizations, and other game systems
  • Prevent abuse, cheating, exploitation, and manipulation of the service
  • Troubleshoot technical problems
  • Enforce Soulwon's Terms of Service
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service

4. Discord

Soulwon operates through Discord. When you interact with the Soulwon Discord bot, Discord may provide information necessary for the bot to respond to your interactions and provide its features.

Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own terms, privacy policy, and other applicable policies. Soulwon does not control how Discord independently processes information through its platform.

5. Game Data

Soulwon may retain game-related information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the game and maintain player progress.

Game data may include collections, cards, print numbers, balances, cooldowns, battle statistics, organization information, and other data generated through your use of Soulwon.

Game data may also be retained when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, resolve disputes, maintain service integrity, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Information Sharing

Soulwon does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure providers that are necessary to operate Soulwon, maintain its database or hosting infrastructure, or provide other essential services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or the rights and safety of others.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with Soulwon. However, no internet-based service, database, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Requests

If you have a question about information associated with your Soulwon account, or would like to make a privacy-related request, contact us at soulwonbot@gmail.com.

When contacting us about account information, we may need sufficient information to determine which Soulwon account or Discord identifier the request concerns.

9. Children's Privacy

Soulwon is provided through Discord and is subject to Discord's age requirements and policies. We do not knowingly request additional personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts outside of what is necessary to operate the service.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Soulwon's features, information practices, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
