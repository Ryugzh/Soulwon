Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Soulwon may process when you use the Soulwon Discord bot and website.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, Soulwon may process Discord identifiers and game-related information such as your Discord user ID, server ID, inventory, cards, prints, balances, cooldowns, battle statistics, and other data required to operate the game.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to provide Soulwon's features, maintain player progress, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, enforce the Terms of Service, and improve the service.

4. Discord

Soulwon operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord's own terms and privacy policy. Soulwon does not control Discord's independent processing of your information.

5. Data retention

Game data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Soulwon, maintain player progress, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate Soulwon, or disclosed when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning information associated with your Soulwon account, contact soulwonbot@gmail.com.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Soulwon's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.
